Machine learning models require large amounts of labeled data to achieve high accuracy in classification tasks. During training, an algorithm adjusts its internal parameters to minimize the difference between predicted and actual outcomes. Overfitting occurs when a model performs well on training data but fails to generalize to new, unseen examples. Techniques such as cross-validation and regularization are commonly used to prevent this problem. As datasets grow larger and more diverse, models tend to become more robust and reliable in real-world applications.
